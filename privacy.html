TipCap Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 · Bundle ID: com.yijia.tipcap

1. Local-First Data Ownership

TipCap stores all shift logs, cash and card tips, workplace settings, paycheck records, and tax profile estimates strictly on your device inside a local encrypted SQLite database. TipCap does not sell, share, or upload your personal financial records to third-party advertising networks.

2. Optional iCloud Backup & Subscriptions

In-App Purchases and auto-renewing subscriptions (TipCap Pro Monthly / Annual) are processed securely by Apple via StoreKit. TipCap never receives or stores your credit card number or Apple ID password. You may manage or cancel subscriptions anytime in iOS Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.

3. Disclaimer

TipCap is an independent personal recordkeeping and payroll verification tool and is not affiliated with the IRS or the U.S. Department of Labor.

TipCap 隐私政策

TipCap 采用本地优先架构，您的班次记录、现金/刷卡小费、门店设置、工资单记录及年度税务估算均安全保存在本机 SQLite 数据库中，不会向任何第三方广告商出售或上传。订阅付款与自动续订由 Apple StoreKit 安全处理，您可随时在 iOS 系统设置中管理或取消订阅。
