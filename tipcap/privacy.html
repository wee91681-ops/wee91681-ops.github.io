TipCap Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 · Bundle ID: com.yijia.tipcap · Zero Data Collection

Core Promise: TipCap operates on a 100% Local-First architecture. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or monetize your personal or financial data. Your shift records, cash tips, and paychecks belong strictly to you.

1. Local-First Data Storage, Receipts & Optional iCloud Backup

All records created within TipCap—including shift logs, cash and card tips, commissions, hourly wages, employer details, business expenses, paycheck audit calculations, and locally compressed receipt photos—are stored strictly on your device inside an encrypted SQLite database and app sandbox. We have no external backend servers storing your financial ledger. If you choose to enable optional iOS iCloud backup in Settings, encrypted snapshots and receipt attachments are stored exclusively in your personal Apple iCloud container, which the developer cannot access.

2. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Subscription payments (TipCap Pro Monthly and Annual) are processed entirely and securely by Apple via iOS StoreKit or by Google via Google Play Billing. TipCap never collects, processes, or stores your credit card details, billing address, Apple ID, or Google account credentials. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time via iOS Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions or Google Play Store > Payments & subscriptions.

3. Optional Device Permissions, Zero Analytics & Zero Advertising

TipCap does not incorporate third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics trackers, or user fingerprinting scripts, and never sells or shares your data. Optional system permissions are requested strictly on demand:

  • Location (When In Use, Optional): Only active when you explicitly start a 1099 business mileage trip log, strictly to accumulate trip distance locally on your device. TipCap stops tracking immediately when you end the trip, never stores route histories, and never uploads GPS coordinates.
  • Camera & Photo Library (Optional): Only used when you choose to photograph or select receipt images for business expenses. Photos are compressed and stored strictly on your device (or your personal iCloud container if enabled).

4. User Rights & Regulatory Compliance

  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA): We collect zero personal information and sell zero consumer data.
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR): Because all data is retained exclusively on your device, you possess total sovereignty to view, export (JSON), or permanently erase your data at any time directly in the app.

5. Disclaimer & Contact

TipCap is an independent personal recordkeeping and FLSA wage verification tool. It is not affiliated with the IRS, state labor boards, or any employer. If you have questions regarding this policy, reach us via email at wee91681@gmail.com or our Support Page.
